# Team Signal Scout (Second Prize)
## Project: Multi-Agent Strategic Intelligence Platform

### ⭐ Key highlight

The most outstanding aspect of this team was not their architecture, but their **ideation process**. They spent considerable time sharing how they identified the right pain points rather than which AWS services to use. This is a mindset highly worth adopting.

---

### 💡 Idea

A Multi-Agent system that assists enterprises in:
* Crawling competitor data.
* Aggregating business signals.
* Analyzing strategic moves.
* Predicting ROI if similar strategies are implemented.

**Workflow:**

```
Competitor -> Financial reports / News / Organization -> AI Agent -> Business Recommendation
```

* **Target customers:** Strategy teams, Enterprises, Risk management.

---

### 📚 Lessons Learned

#### 1. Technology cannot triumph over the Problem
Perhaps the best quote of the day: *"No matter how cool the technology is, it cannot exceed the boundaries of business requirements."* This is an aspect many students tend to overlook.

#### 2. Concept is more important than complete code
In a hackathon, you do not need 100% completion. What matters is identifying the right problem, defining the correct target customer, and having a working demo.

#### 3. MVP > Perfect
They emphasized that many teams fail due to scope creep, ending up with unfinished code and no demo. It is much better to have a 20% scope that runs than a 100% scope that does not.

#### 4. Teamwork
They shared honest stories about arguments, brainstorming sessions, and setting aside egos to align on a single direction. This is a common path for almost any hackathon team.

#### 5. What do the Judges care about?
An interesting insight: Judges asking many questions is not a bad sign; it means they are highly engaged. You should worry if they ask nothing at all.

---

### 💬 Presentation Feelings

This was my favorite session regarding product and team mindset. It did not focus on bragging about technology but instead shared valuable battle-tested experiences on ideation, pitching, teamwork, MVP, and scope management. If you are preparing for a hackathon, this is a must-listen.
