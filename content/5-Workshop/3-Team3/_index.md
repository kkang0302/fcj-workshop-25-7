# Team BL
## Project: AI Solution Architect Assistant

### ⭐ Key highlight

They started from a practical workplace pain point: Customers often request architecture design, cost estimation, and deployment options in an extremely short timeframe. This is a very common challenge faced by Solution Architects (SA).

---

### 💡 Idea

An AI system that takes natural language requirements and corporate policies as input, then automatically generates architecture diagrams, cost estimations, and Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) templates (Terraform / CloudFormation), and supports automated deployment.

**Workflow:**

```
Requirement -> AI -> Architecture / Cost / IaC (Terraform/CloudFormation) -> Deploy
```

---

### 📚 Lessons Learned

#### 1. AI should aim to boost productivity
AI does not replace SAs. Instead, it slashes prep time from **2 days to 30 minutes**, allowing the Solution Architect to focus on reviews and optimization.

#### 2. Context is king
The team allowed uploading organizational policies, guidelines, and company standards. This ensures that the generated architecture aligns perfectly with corporate compliance.

#### 3. End-to-end automation
Instead of stopping at drawing diagrams or estimating costs, they pushed the solution further by generating IaC and enabling automated provisioning. This is a complete Agentic workflow.

---

### 💬 Presentation Feelings

The idea is incredibly powerful, especially for anyone who has worked in cloud infrastructure or SA roles.
